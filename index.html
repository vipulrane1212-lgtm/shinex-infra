Frequently Asked Questions

Before the first site visit

Do you only design, or do you also execute? +

Both. Design, civil work, carpentry, and finish are executed by our in-house team. You don't have to coordinate five separate contractors or manage disputes between architects and site masons.

Can you visit my site in Seawoods or Nerul? +

Yes. Complimentary site visits are the standard first step. We bring laser measuring tools, review your society guidelines, and discuss layout options on the spot.

What is the typical turnaround for a 2 or 3 BHK? +

A finish and millwork interior usually completes in 35–45 days. A complete civil overhaul (including bathroom retiling, wall alterations, and electrical revamping) typically spans 60–75 days with a guaranteed written schedule.

Do you handle bathroom waterproofing and plumbing? +

Yes. Wet areas are a core civil strength. We perform multi-layer chemical waterproofing and pressure-test plumbing lines before laying tiles to prevent any leakage issues down the road.
