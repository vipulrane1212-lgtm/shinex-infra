Sequential Cadence

Overview of the 5 Phases

Step 01

Initial Brief

We review your flat's layout, CIDCO society restrictions, lifestyle requirements, storage goals, and aesthetic vision.

Step 02

Site Measure

Physical visit with laser distancemeters. We record column offsets, beam drops, plumbing shafts, and core dampness levels.

Step 03

Design & BOQ

Detailed 3D renders, moodboard tactile approval, electrical schematics, and an itemized BOQ with guaranteed line-item costs.

Step 04

Civil & Millwork

Concealed conduits, multi-coat waterproofing, tile masonry, and precision millwork fabricated by our in-house carpenters.

Step 05

Snag & Handover

Joint inspection, punch-list clearance, industrial deep cleaning, warranty documentation, and key handover ready to live in.
