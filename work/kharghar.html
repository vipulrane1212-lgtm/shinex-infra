Case Study · Kharghar Hills

Kharghar Veranda

Weatherproof outdoor transition: Kota stone, natural teak slatted seating, concealed drainage channels, and tropical planter alcoves.

LocationSector 20, Kharghar
ConfigurationBalcony & Veranda Suite
ScopeWaterproofing, Masonry & Joinery
Key MaterialsRough-Honed Kota, Teak & Brass

Monsoon Waterproofing & Materials

Kharghar's heavy monsoon downpours require strict slope correction, concealed overflow drains, and UV-resistant outdoor finishes. We laid anti-skid Kota stone with epoxy grouting to prevent efflorescence, paired with marine-varnished plantation teak seating.
