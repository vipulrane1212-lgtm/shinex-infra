NRI Complex Residence

A serene coastal language: low travertine coffee tables, bespoke fluted oak credenza, frameless fluted glass wet areas, and unlacquered brass hardware.

LocationNRI Complex Phase 1 & 2
Configuration3 BHK Coastal Flat
ScopeFull Civil, Waterproofing & Finish
Key MaterialsOak, Limestone, Fluted Glass, Brass

Coastal Apartment Considerations

Being positioned right along the Thane Creek and Seawoods wetlands, NRI Complex units require marine-grade plywood (IS:710), multi-coat anti-fungal waterproofing, and anti-corrosive brass or stainless hardware. Our turnkey team handles all approvals with the NRI Complex management society directly.
